— A LITTLE ABOUT BLOGSPACE

Big ideas begin
with small beginnings.

BlogSpace is a welcoming home for ideas in progress. It's a place to share what you're learning, document your journey, explain something you love, or simply put a thought into words.

We believe writing isn't only for experts. A short lesson from a beginner can help another beginner; a personal observation can spark a conversation; and a project recap can make the next person's path a little easier.

Our missionMake sharing ideas feel simple, useful and welcoming.
Our visionA community where curiosity matters more than perfection.
Our valuesBe thoughtful, stay open-minded, and give credit to learning.
Built for creatorsWrite, discover, discuss and save stories in one place.

Made with: HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Node.js · Express · JSON storage

— GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things you
might be wondering.

New here? Here's the short version.

What can I publish on BlogSpace?＋

Share tutorials, project notes, design ideas, career lessons, personal reflections, or anything useful and respectful that you'd like others to read.

Do I need an account to read stories?＋

You can browse and search stories without signing in. An account is needed to publish, like, bookmark, or comment.

Can I edit or remove my story?＋

Yes. Sign in and open a story you wrote to find the edit and delete options. Please review carefully before deleting because that action cannot be undone.

How do saved stories work?＋

Use the save icon on a story to bookmark it. Sign in and choose “Saved stories” to find your reading list again.

What is this project built with?＋

The interface uses HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The server runs on Node.js and Express, with JSON-file storage for this demo so a separate database server is not required.
